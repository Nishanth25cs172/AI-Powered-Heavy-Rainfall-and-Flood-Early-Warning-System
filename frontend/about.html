About System

Comprehensive Overview of the AI-Powered Heavy Rainfall & Flood Early-Warning Platform.

🛡️ SYSTEM OVERVIEW & PURPOSE

The AI-Powered Heavy Rainfall & Flood Early-Warning System provides real-time disaster intelligence by integrating meteorological observations with hydrological watershed telemetry. The system enables public safety authorities to anticipate extreme weather hazards and issue actionable alerts before flooding occurs.

Key Core Deliverables:
  • Precipitation forecasting using historical time-series data.
  • Inundation probability risk assessment via Random Forest models.
  • Geospatial GIS risk mapping across critical river basins.
  • Automated early warning sirens & advisory protocols.
🧠 AI / ML MODELING APPROACH

The platform utilizes dual machine learning classifiers trained on verified historical datasets to deliver high-precision predictions:

Rainfall Model: Logistic Regression
Analyzes rolling 7-day precipitation trends (Lag1, Lag3, Lag7) and regional monsoon indices.
Flood Risk Model: Random Forest Classifier
Evaluates 13 hydraulic parameters including water stage levels, river discharge, soil moisture, slope, and land cover.
