  • ● Target: Flood Occurred (Binary Inundation Signal)

🚨 Mandatory Evacuation Checklist

  • 1
    Disconnect Main Power & Gas:

    Prevent electrical shock hazards and gas leaks before water ingress.

  • 2
    Move to Designated High-Ground:

    Follow designated municipal evacuation routes; avoid low underpasses and culverts.

  • 3
    Assemble Emergency Survival Kit:

    Pack 72-hour potable water, dry rations, torch, power bank, first-aid, and identity proofs.

  • 4
    Maintain Battery-Powered Radio / Telemetry Contact:

    Tune to local DDMA/SDMA emergency broadcasts for clearance updates.
